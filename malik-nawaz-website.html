My Story

40 Years of Walking These Streets, Knowing Every Lane

I started this work back in the early 1980s, when Bismillah Colony was still being built street by street. My uncle was a property dealer, and I used to ride with him on his cycle to show plots to buyers — I was just a teenager then. I watched deals happen on handshakes, trust was everything. That's still how I work today.

In four decades, I have seen this city grow from a small industrial town to one of Pakistan's largest cities. I know which colonies have water issues, which areas get flooded in monsoon, which streets have good resale value — the kind of things no online listing will ever tell you. When I give you advice, I'm giving you 40 years of lived experience in Faisalabad, not a sales pitch.

My approach is simple: I only show you properties I would personally consider for my own family. If a deal is not good for you, I will say so — even if it means losing a commission. That honesty is why people have been sending their relatives and friends to me for generations. I don't advertise. My clients are my advertisement.
